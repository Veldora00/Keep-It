Privacy Policy for Keep It

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Keep It ("the app") is a personal budgeting app. This page explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Account

The app requires an account (email and password) to sign in. Your account is used only to identify which transactions and settings belong to you.

What data the app stores

When you add a transaction by hand, or import one from a bank/card statement CSV file you choose to upload, the app stores: the transaction description/merchant name, amount, date, category, and whether it repeats. This data is stored in a Supabase database used by the app, associated with your account.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and does not use advertising or analytics tracking of any kind.

How your bank statement data is used

When you import a CSV file, the app reads it only on your device to extract the transaction rows above. The file itself is never uploaded anywhere — only the resulting transaction description, amount, date and type are stored, the same as if you had typed them in by hand.

AI categorization

To help suggest a spending category for a transaction the app's built-in rules can't already classify, the transaction's description and amount (never your name, email, or account details) may be sent to OpenAI's API for a one-word category suggestion. You always see and can change the suggested category before it is saved.

Data sharing and sale

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. It is only used to provide the app's own budgeting features to you.

Data deletion

You can delete individual transactions at any time within the app. To delete your account and all associated data entirely, contact the email below.
