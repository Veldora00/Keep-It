Delete your Keep It account and data

This page explains how to request deletion of your Keep It account and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to milan.lama0626@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Keep It account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Keep It, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will delete your account and confirm by reply within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your sign-in account (email address) and everything saved under it: transactions, categories, goals, habits and habit logs, and any loan details you entered.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you is kept after deletion. Routine encrypted backups held by our database provider are overwritten on their normal cycle.

Delete some data without closing your account

You can delete individual transactions at any time inside the app, without deleting your account.
