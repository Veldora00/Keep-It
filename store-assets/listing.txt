SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 chars):
Track spending, spot habits, and see where your money actually goes.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars):
Keep It is a straightforward personal budgeting app for tracking what you spend and what you earn — without spreadsheets.

• Import your bank or credit card statement as a CSV and Keep It sorts your transactions into categories automatically, so you're not typing every purchase in by hand.
• See your balance, this month's income and spending, and a breakdown by category at a glance.
• Track everyday habits (coffee, takeout, subscriptions) and see exactly what they're costing you over time.
• Forecast where your balance is headed based on your recurring income and bills — no manual setup required, Keep It finds the patterns in your own history.
• Refunds and cancelled purchases are automatically netted out, so your spending totals reflect what you actually kept, not the raw transaction list.

Keep It doesn't show ads, doesn't sell your data, and doesn't track you across other apps. It's built to answer one question honestly: where is your money actually going?
